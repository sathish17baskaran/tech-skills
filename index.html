📣 LinkedIn content kit

A simple, repeatable format for short, scannable posts. Every section has ready drafts in the blue "LinkedIn post idea" boxes (click Copy).

1. Hook (line 1)

A question, a myth or an analogy. "Queue vs Event in one line 📬📢"

2. Simple explanation

An everyday analogy: coffee buzzer, hotel key card, fuse box.

3. 3–5 bullets

Emoji bullets, one idea each. No jargon without a definition.

4. Visual

Screenshot a diagram from these pages, or make a carousel of 5–8 slides.

5. Takeaway + question

"Rule of thumb: … Which do you use? 👇"

6. 3–5 hashtags

#SystemDesign #Integration #APIs #GenAI #Architecture

  • Keep it under ~1,300 characters. Use short lines and white space.
  • One concept per post. Turn "X vs Y" topics into a series.
  • Add a real lesson from your own work. It beats textbook content every time (leave out confidential details).
