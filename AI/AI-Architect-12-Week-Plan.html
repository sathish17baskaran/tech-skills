Exams

  • Book AI-103 and/or AIP-C01 for weeks 13–14
  • Then target AI-500 (Multi-Agent AI Solutions Expert)
  • Optional governance: IAPP AIGP

Weekly Rhythm (~11 hrs)

  • 1 hr – watch the ⭐ "Start here" video and one more video
  • 3 hrs – courses
  • 5 hrs – hands-on lab on the running project
  • 1 hr – official docs and architecture reading
  • 1 hr – write-up (ADR / diagram / blog) to build your architect portfolio

Tips

  • Watch videos first for the big picture, then use the docs as the source of truth.
  • Pick one primary cloud: go deep on Week 9 or 10 and keep the other as comparison.
  • DeepLearning.AI short courses, Hugging Face, Microsoft GitHub curricula, Microsoft Learn and all videos are free. Coursera and some Skill Builder labs need a subscription.
  • If a link has moved, search the title on the provider's site or YouTube.
